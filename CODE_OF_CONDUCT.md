# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in QueryWeave a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints,
accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, trolling, insulting comments,
unwelcome sexual attention, publishing private information, and conduct that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report
unacceptable behavior through the private channel described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers will
review reports promptly, respect the reporter's privacy, and take proportionate corrective action.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
